Getting there

Glamping spots are down country roads, so we need at least one car. The easy plan: the NYC crew rents a car, and our Boston friend takes Amtrak and gets picked up at the station, or drives and meets us there.

🗽 From NYC

Leave by noon Friday or after 7 pm to miss the worst of I-95 and Merritt Parkway traffic. Splitting one rental is cheaper than train tickets plus a car up there.

🦞 From Boston

No car? Amtrak's Northeast Regional runs from Boston to Mystic, New London and Old Saybrook in about 1.5 to 2 hours. We can grab you from the station on the way in.
