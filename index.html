Where in Connecticut?

Four regions, each about two hours from both cities. The bars show drive time with no traffic; add 45 to 60 minutes on a Friday afternoon. Tap one to filter the stays below.

🧭

Fairest drive: the Connecticut River Valley. Chester and East Haddam are about 2 hours from both Manhattan and Boston, with Gillette Castle, waterfalls and kayaking nearby. Most glamping for the money: the Underhill Hollow treehouse in the Quiet Corner sleeps all 4 for about $90 each, but NYC drives closer to 3 hours. Good middle ground: the Water Forest treehouse + tiny house near Norwich, about 2h15 from NYC and 1h40 from Boston.

More options

Real tents & yurts, beyond Airbnb

Heads up: most true glamping tents and yurts in Connecticut sleep 2, and many aren't on Airbnb. If we want canvas over our heads instead of a cabin roof, these are the places to look. Book early, summer weekends go fast. Most glamping sites run roughly May through October.

Logistics

Getting there

Most glamping spots are down country roads, so we'll want at least one car. The easiest plan: NYC crew rents a car, Boston friend takes the train and gets picked up, or drives and meets us there.

🗽 From NYC (3 of us)

Leave by noon Friday or after 7 pm to dodge the worst of I-95 / Merritt Parkway traffic. Splitting a rental 3 ways is usually cheaper than three train tickets plus a car up there.

🦞 From Boston (1 of us)

No car? Amtrak's Northeast Regional runs Boston → Mystic, New London and Old Saybrook in about 1.5 to 2 hours. We can grab them from the station on the way in.
